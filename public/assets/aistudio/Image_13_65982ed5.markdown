## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Narrative
This design system embodies maternal physical and emotional rehabilitation in the comfort of home. Grounded in the ethos of *"Armonía entre fuerza, salud y maternidad desde el hogar"*, the visual language balances clinical credibility with profound empathy and domestic warmth. Postpartum care demands an environment devoid of sterile hospital detachment or aggressive fitness tropes; it requires nurturing reassurance, measured pacing, and quiet strength.

### Emotional Resonance
The UI evokes feelings of safety, steady physical restoration, non-judgmental accompaniment, and serene clarity. Users navigating sleep deprivation and bodily vulnerability should encounter zero visual friction, abrasive contrasts, or cognitive overload.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **warm organic minimalism** with **tactile softness**. Surfaces are warm linen, cream, and unbleached porcelain rather than cold electronic whites. Structural boundaries rely on delicate tonal shifts and warm hairline dividers reminiscent of anatomical golden-ratio geometries, reflecting the spiral motif and feminine human movement captured in the emblem.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette draws straight from natural pigments, earthen pottery, botanical calm, and human skin tones:

- **Primary (`#B85D43` / Coral Clay / Terracotta):** Represents vital inner warmth, resilience, and pelvic core rebuilding. Used for key call-to-actions, energetic milestones, and interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#5B8266` / Botanical Sage):** Evokes healing, breathwork, calm rehabilitation, and restorative physical therapy. Ideal for completed states, clinical validation tags, restorative exercise markers, and gentle alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#E3A896` / Soft Shell Coral):** A delicate tint for active highlights, background chips, badge backdrops, and progress track fills.
- **Neutral Base (`#FBF8F3` Canvas, `#F4EFEB` Surface Container, `#FFFFFF` Card Elevated):** Rich, unbleached almond, parchment, and warm sand. Avoids cold `#000000` or sterile `#F8F9FA`.
- **Text & Foreground (`#2D2926` Primary Slate Umber, `#57514B` Secondary Warm Muted):** Deep charcoal with warm umber undertones to maintain contrast ratios compliant with WCAG AAA while eliminating harsh retinal glare.

## Typography

### Editorial & Humanist Pairing
The typographic hierarchy balances literary dignity with clean legibility:

- **Display & Section Titles (`Newsreader`):** An organic, sculpted serif font that captures the grace and timeless dignity of motherhood. Used for motivational greetings, clinical program titles, and mindfulness prompts.
- **Body, UI, & Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A soft, humanist geometric sans-serif featuring open counters and balanced curves. It offers effortless legibility for tired eyes, whether scanning postpartum exercise instructions, symptom trackers, or scheduled routines.
- **Numerals & Metrics:** Large metrics (e.g., minutes rested, pelvic floor cycle counts) use `Newsreader` in medium weight for an artisanal, deliberate feeling rather than an intimidating gym stopwatch aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

### Mobile-First Fluid Harmony
Designed primarily as an adaptive web application optimized for one-handed mobile use while holding a baby:

- **Column System:** 
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` screen margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive triggers and primary CTAs are locked to the comfortable thumb zone at the screen base.
  - **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins, allowing split modules for exercise guidance alongside clinical notes.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** Max container width capped at `1040px` centered to maintain intimacy and prevent wide eye tracking fatigue.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical breathing room (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) prevents sensory overload. Elements do not compete for space; cards maintain comfortable internal padding (`1.25rem` to `1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Warmth & Diffused Layering
The design rejects standard cold drop shadows and harsh skeuomorphism in favor of soft ambient warmth:

- **Surface Tiers:** 
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FBF8F3` (warm parchment linen).
  - **Container Low:** `#F4EFEB` (recessed sections, exercise breakdown trays).
  - **Elevated Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (pristine yet warm white).
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** Tinted with warm umber rather than cold pitch black.
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(87, 81, 75, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(87, 81, 75, 0.03);`
  - *Floating Modal / Active Sheet:* `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.04);`
- **Hairline Framing:** Subtle borders using `rgba(91, 130, 102, 0.12)` or `rgba(184, 93, 67, 0.12)` create delicate, intentional outlines reminiscent of the golden ratio guiding lines in the brand mark.

## Shapes

### Organic Curvilinear Language
Reflecting the spiral and maternal anatomy from the identity:
- **Component Radii:** A base corner radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`) scales up to `1rem` (`16px`) for cards and interactive panels, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) for wellness containers and bottom drawer sheets.
- **Full Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved for badges, status chips, routine category selectors, and primary action buttons, conveying touchable softness without sharp corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full-pill shaped, background `#B85D43`, text `#FFFFFF`, with high-contrast font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600 weight. Soft micro-scaling (`transform: scale(0.98)`) and warm shadow expansion on active press.
- **Secondary (Restorative/Clinical):** Full-pill, soft Sage outline `border: 1.5px solid #5B8266`, background transparent or `#5B8266` at 8% opacity, text `#40634D`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text `#57514B`, subtle warm underline or background `#F4EFEB` on hover.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Pill contours with gentle background washes.
- *Clinical Tag:* Background `#5B8266` at 12% opacity, text `#40634D`, paired with a petite dot indicator.
- *Trimester / Recovery Week:* Background `#B85D43` at 10% opacity, text `#B85D43`.

### Interactive Cards
- `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FBF8F3` base. Generous `1.25rem` padding, `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`), surrounded by subtle warm ambient shadow.
- Header incorporates `Newsreader` titles with metadata chips docked at the top right.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with a warm border `1px solid rgba(87, 81, 75, 0.2)`. Focus state smoothly transitions the ring to `#B85D43` with a soft 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(184, 93, 67, 0.15)`). Label placed above in `label-md` uppercase warm umber.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- Rounded organic check handles. Checked state shifts from muted neutral to filled `#5B8266` (Sage Green) for health tracking habits, accompanied by a clean cream-colored check icon.
- Toggles feature an elongated capsule with a gentle warm stone thumb that slides smoothly.

### Gentle Progress States & Trackers
- Pelvic rehabilitation and recovery bars use smooth, rounded end-caps. Track background is `#F4EFEB`, while the active fill features a fluid gradient from `#E3A896` to `#B85D43` (or calm `#5B8266` for rest/breathwork modules).
- Daily recovery radial trackers employ thin, elegant track strokes reminiscent of the emblem's golden spiral.
